Symphonic Rock For A Noble Cause

Learn more about the vision behind Beats of Beirut, our organizers, and the lives we aim to transform through music.

The Vision

Organized proudly by the Rotary Club of Beirut Cedars, "We Will Rock You" Symphonic Rock Concert is born out of a desire to merge cultural excellence with impactful social responsibility.

By blending iconic rock anthems with a full master class symphony orchestra, we create a high-octane sensory environment that unites rock enthusiasts and classical music lovers alike under one roof at the prestigious Casino du Liban.

Supporting AFEL

All net proceeds from this breathtaking performance go directly to support AFEL (Association Foyer de l'Enfant Libanais).

For decades, AFEL has been a beacon of hope in Lebanon, dedicated to assisting, protecting, and providing comprehensive psychological, educational, and social rehabilitation to vulnerable children facing abuse, neglect, or extreme socio-economic hardships. Your ticket purchase directly contributes to building their brighter tomorrow.

Merging the raw energy of rock performance with orchestral majesty for a powerful social cause.
