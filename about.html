Our Story

About Beats of Beirut

A visionary initiative bridging cultural heritage and futuristic sonic landscapes.

The Vision

Beats of Beirut was born out of a desire to showcase the resilience, creativity, and evolution of our city's musical identity. By blending classical orchestral arrangements with modern electronic synth elements, we create an immersive artistic experience that honors our past while pioneering the future.

The Experience

This is more than a concert series—it's a cultural movement. From large-scale live performances featuring custom light setups to collaborative digital platforms supporting local underground producers, we provide an ecosystem where art meets innovation.

Bridging classical orchestration with cutting-edge electronic audio synthesis.
