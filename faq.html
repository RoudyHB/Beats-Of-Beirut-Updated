Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ticketing, venues, and event safety protocols.

Where is the event located?

Our main concert installations take place at iconic cultural heritage sites across Beirut. Detailed location pins and parking directions are emailed directly to ticket holders two weeks before the show date.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable but are fully transferable to another person. If an event is postponed due to unexpected regional developments, your ticket automatically remains valid for the rescheduled date.

What safety measures are in place?

Your safety is our priority. Every venue features clearly marked exits, dedicated medical stations, professional security teams, and secure, well-lit perimeter access routes.
