Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing tickets, venue policies, and seating arrangements.

Where can I purchase tickets for the event?

Tickets are explicitly sold online through the Virgin Ticketing Box Office. You can access the seat selector directly by clicking any of the book tickets buttons on our Home page.

What is the exact location and gate timing?

The concert takes place inside the grand auditorium at Casino du Liban on Thursday, May 29. Doors open for the reception at 07:30 PM, and the curtain rises promptly at 08:30 PM.

How are the ticket sales proceeds managed?

Beats of Beirut is entirely a charity event. All net proceeds collected by the Rotary Club of Beirut Cedars are audited and paid out completely to AFEL to fund childhood safety, support, and welfare programs.

Merging the raw energy of rock performance with orchestral majesty for a powerful social cause.
